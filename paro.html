THE GATEWAY

Begin where Bhutan's story rises.

Paro is the natural first chapter of a Bhutan journey: the country's international gateway, a wide green valley, and home to some of its most treasured sacred and historic sites.

Give yourself time to settle into the altitude and let the valley unfold beyond the famous viewpoints.

HIGHLIGHTS

Paro, beyond the postcard.

Tiger's Nest

Hike to Paro Taktsang, Bhutan's iconic cliffside monastery and most celebrated sacred site.

Rinpung Dzong

Walk through one of Bhutan's finest dzongs and take in views over the Paro Valley.

Kyichu Lhakhang

Visit one of Bhutan's oldest temples and a quiet anchor for reflection.

SUGGESTED ITINERARY

Three days in Paro.

Day 1

Arrive at Paro International Airport, settle into the valley and visit Rinpung Dzong.

Day 2

Make the Tiger's Nest hike, then rest with a traditional hot-stone experience.

Day 3

Explore Kyichu Lhakhang, Drukgyel Dzong and the villages around Paro.

TRAVEL NOTES

Good to know.

When is the best time to visit Paro?

Spring and autumn usually offer comfortable hiking conditions, clear mountain views and lively cultural calendars.

How difficult is the Tiger's Nest hike?

It is a demanding half-day hike with steady uphill sections. Most travelers can enjoy it with breaks and a sensible pace.

How long should I stay?

Two to three days gives you time for Tiger's Nest, the valley's heritage sites and a slower local experience.
